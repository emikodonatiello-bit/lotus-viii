Introduction

Welcome to my digital garden, where my goal is to preserve my thoughts beyond my existence. Here is a maze in which lies many doors; and through those doors and on the way to them, you can see how one person may think; what does one find beautiful, what does one take interest in, what does one wonder, what does one believe? Inside here is a culmination of how many people can shape a person, and acts as an example of how one person may navigate the digital world. Here lies my memories, and here lies what we’ve made from letters and numbers.

Why Lotus? A lotus is a beautiful flower that can grow from mud. Some people use that as a motivation for resilience. To me, it symbolizes tranquility and balance; a flower is able to float upon the delicate surface of water. It reminds me of Buddhist teachings, projected across the room on the second floor of the buddhist church in chinatown. It reminds me of that place with the curly-haired pastor and the hair-less monk who rang a bell or hit a cymbal or something like that. It reminds me of the benches–pews–and the thick purple book in white letters with the gridded texture on the cover. From there we would chant hiragana which has only lost its written meaning, but never spiritual. Always something about the depths of the doctrine, and maybe a song or something. But rarely could we look others in the eye there and know; know how it was to be yonsei, gosei, rokusei… We’d never know Manzanar, Tule Lake, Minidoka as well. We’d been the ones to trail behind when they had already paved the road ahead.

Why a Garden? A garden is a beautiful place where many things grow, and many things may coexist. A garden also because I had read or I had heard of the term, “digital garden”. The way the letters sound in my head–a familiar voice with no voice–made me able to name what I have been wanting to make.

Who is this for? This is for me, but also maybe my children, my grandchildren, my great-grandchildren… I originally wanted to have a central place where I could realize my thoughts in a way that would be satisfying to look at. Outside, I am a messy person, but inside–I am very much the same. And so, I decided to organize all of my stuff so that I would be able to do work, track projects, write my thoughts, etc. in a way that is accessible to me while also exploring my interests and hobbies.
